# 🚀 Costco Australia Scraper

Costco Australia Scraper is a **free and open-source** scraper that gets you **unlimited** detailed Costco Australia data for free.

## ✨ What Can I Get?

- 🛒 **Full details of 6,000+ products** — price, discount & sale dates, stock level, ratings & reviews, barcodes, specs, images & variants
- 🔥 **400+ Hot Buys, plus new arrivals & last-chance deals** — savings, discount %, end dates, ratings and stock for every deal
- 📂 **All 28 departments & 1,300+ categories** — the full category tree with codes and links, plus every product in any category
- ⛽ **All 15 Costco warehouses** — address, phone, opening & holiday hours, services and fuel prices (E10, Unleaded 91, Premium 98, Diesel)

## 📚 Endpoints

10 endpoints cover everything you need.

| Endpoint | Path | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| Product Details | `/products/details` | The whole product in one call: price, stock, ratings, specs |
| Search Suggestions | `/products/suggestions` | What Costco's own search box suggests as you type |
| Search Products | `/products/search` | Any product by keyword, with brand and facet filters |
| Bulk Products | `/products/bulk` | Up to 30 products in one call, members-only prices included |
| Product Reviews | `/products/reviews` | Every review with pros, cons, photos and rating stats |
| Deals | `/deals/list` | Hot Buys, new arrivals and last-chance deals with savings |
| Categories / Category Products | `/categories/list`, `/categories/products` | The full category tree and every product in any category |
| Warehouses / Warehouse Details | `/stores/list`, `/stores/details` | All 15 warehouses with hours, services and fuel prices |
